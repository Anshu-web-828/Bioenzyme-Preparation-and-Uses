Informations:

What is Bioenzyme?

A Bioenzyme, also known as a garbage-enzyme, is an enzyme produced from kitchen and organic wastes, such as vegetable peels, fruit peels, dried leaves, floers, fruits, etc. It is a non-expensive and safe alternative to many of the chemicals in use today.

Preparation of Bioenzymes

A Bioenzyme can be prepared easily by anyone sitting in their homes. We can easily make bioenzymes by using peels of fruits, and vegetables, and dried leaves, flowers, fruits, etc., plastic bottles, water and sugar/jaggery.

Procedures

The steps included in preparing a Bioenzyme are as follows:

i. Take some plastic bottles,

ii. Take the kitchen waste or dried fruits, leaves, and flowers,

iii. Take some jaggery/sugar,

iv. Pour the wastes and jaggery/sugar in the plastic bottle,

v. Fill the bottle with water,

vi. Fill the bottle in the ratio 1:3:10

vii. Close the bottle tightly,

viii. Keep the bottles in a shady and warm area, and

ix. Open the lid to release the gas formed inside the bottle due to fermentation.

Precautions and Safety Measures

There are chances of the bottles exploding due to excessive gas formation due to the process of fermentation. This is the reason that some precautions and safety measures need to be taken before preparing them. The precations and safety measures are given below:

i. Consider using a plastic bottle because it can expand upto some extent before the explosion (if not taken care about the gas releasation). Other bottles can't expand, so they will burst before plastic bottles can, and can cause more harm and injuries.

ii. Keep some space empty at the top of the bottle to allow the gas to stay for some time.

iii. Don't use a wide-necked bottle.

iv. Don't keep the lid open. This will not let the process of fermentation to take place.

Uses

The uses of bioenzymes are listed below:

i. For cleaning purposes.

ii. For room refreshener.

iii. For repelling mosquitoes.

iv. For washing dishes.

v. As manure in plants.

Concerns

The preparation process for bioenzymes have certain concerns. These are:

i. The bottles may burst if not taken care of.

ii. The gas at the beginning may be bad smelling, i.e., the smell won't be good.

iii. The enzyme is concentrated, mix it with water(about 1 cap enzyme for 1 litre water) to dilute it and then use it.

The ratio:

The ratio 1:3:10 is used to prevent various accidents including the bursting of bottles. It is also used to ferment the waste faster and prepare the enzyme faster.

- The part of '1' is used to represent the jaggery or sugar used to speed up the Fermentation process.

- The part of '3' is used for the waste that is to be Fermented.

- The part of '10' is used for the water needed for the process of Fermmentation to occur.

Fermentation

Fermentation is a biological process in which microorganisms, such as yeast and bacteria, convert sugars into simpler substances like alcohol, acids, and gases, usually in the absence of oxygen.

Examples of fermentation:

- Bread-making: Yeast converts sugar into carbon dioxide, which makes the dough rise.

- Curd formation: Bacteria convert the milk sugar (lactose) into lactic acid, which turns milk into curd.

- Alcohol production: Yeast converts sugar into alcohol and carbon dioxide.
